## Anatomy

A `<swc-menu>` consists of:

- An externally-referenced **trigger**, set via the `for` or `triggerElement` property. `swc-menu` does not render a trigger of its own.
- A shadow-internal surface carrying `role="menu"`, anchored to the trigger.
- **Default slot**: the menu's rows. `swc-menu-item` has not shipped yet, so these examples slot placeholder rows with `role="menuitem"` until it does.

## Options

### Sizes

`size` accepts `s`, `m`, `l`, and `xl` (default `m`). `swc-menu-item` doesn't exist yet, so no row content currently reflects this size; set it now so your markup carries the right value once row-level sizing ships.

## Behaviors

### Open and close

Clicking the trigger opens the menu and moves focus to the first row. From there:

- <kbd>Arrow Up</kbd> / <kbd>Arrow Down</kbd> move focus between rows, wrapping
  from the last row back to the first and vice versa.
- <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> are trapped on the active
  row; they do not move focus out of the menu.
- <kbd>Enter</kbd> or a click on a row closes the menu.
- <kbd>Escape</kbd> or a click outside the menu closes it and returns focus to
  the trigger.

The menu is a native `popover="auto"` element: it renders in the browser's top layer, so it is never clipped by an ancestor's `overflow`, and Escape/outside-click light-dismiss come from the platform rather than a hand-rolled listener.

Four events mark the lifecycle: `swc-open` and `swc-after-open` fire as the menu opens; `swc-close` and `swc-after-close` fire as it closes, however the close was triggered.

## Accessibility

### Features

#### ARIA role and relationship

1. **`role="menu"`**: set on the shadow-internal surface.
2. **`aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded`**: wired onto the trigger's interactive element (for a 2nd-gen SWC trigger with an open shadow root, its inner `<button>`) as the menu opens and closes.
3. **Accessible name**: `swc-menu` has no `label` property. The menu's accessible name comes entirely from the trigger you author; give the trigger its own accessible name (visible text, `aria-label`, or `accessible-label` on a 2nd-gen SWC trigger).

#### Keyboard navigation

- <kbd>Arrow Up</kbd> / <kbd>Arrow Down</kbd>: move focus between rows, wrapping
  at the ends.
- <kbd>Tab</kbd> / <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd>: trapped on the active row
  while the menu is open.
- <kbd>Enter</kbd>: activates the focused row and closes the menu.
- <kbd>Escape</kbd>: closes the menu and returns focus to the trigger.

### Best practices

- **Give the trigger an accessible name.** The menu has no accessible-name property of its own; an unlabeled trigger leaves the whole menu unlabeled too.
- **Don't slot interactive descendants.** Rows carry text and, once `swc-menu-item` ships, decorative icons only. Slotting a button, link, or other focusable element inside a row breaks the roving-tabindex model.
