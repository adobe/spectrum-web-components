## Anatomy

An asset consists of:

- **Default slot**: a single `<img>` or `<svg>` element to display

The asset automatically centers its content both horizontally and vertically within the available space.

<Canvas of={AssetStories.Anatomy} />

## Options

### Fit

`fit` controls how slotted content fills the asset's box: `cover` (default) scales content to fill
the box, cropping if needed; `contain` scales content to fit entirely within the box, letterboxing
if the aspect ratios differ.

<Canvas of={AssetStories.Fit} />

### Background

`background` controls what shows behind slotted content and around its edges when `fit="contain"`
letterboxes it: `transparent` (default), a `solid` color, or an opacity `checkerboard`.

Consuming components are responsible for applying other styles such as borders, shadows, and corner rounding to the asset.

<Canvas of={AssetStories.Background} />

## Behaviors

### Sizing

Set `aspect-ratio`, or `width`/`height`, to size the asset. `aspect-ratio` accepts CSS `<ratio>`
syntax (including the `square` keyword) and has no effect when both `width` and `height` are set.

<Canvas of={AssetStories.Sizing} />

## Accessibility

### Features

The `<swc-asset>` element implements several accessibility features:

#### ARIA implementation

- **Non-interactive**: assets have no interactive behavior and are not focusable

### Best practices

- Provide a meaningful `alt` attribute on a slotted `<img>`, or `aria-label` (with `role="img"`) on a slotted `<svg>`
- For decorative images, use an empty `alt=""` attribute on the img tag
