## Anatomy

`swc-card-view-waterfall-loop` places 50 unequal-height cards into the shortest available visual column. Each card contains a Picsum photo, selection checkbox, primary action, and Share action. The collection exposes one semantic row with 50 gridcells, independent of the visual layout.

## Behaviors

### Playground

Arrow keys move spatially to enabled neighbors. At an edge, focus loops to the opposite edge of the overlapping visual band or column. Horizontal candidates must overlap vertically; vertical candidates must overlap horizontally. Waterfall bands are geometric relationships, not aligned horizontal rows or additional semantic rows.

## Accessibility

We recommend a single-row grid for collection-position feedback such as "x of y." Stable one-based column indices describe semantic position, while visual geometry determines movement in this experiment. Screen reader announcements still need verification.

Test whether looping across an overlapping band is predictable when waterfall cards have different heights. Sighted users see two-dimensional neighbors; users relying on semantics encounter one sequence and may not anticipate the same destination. The horizontal-neighbor problem remains open because waterfall cards do not share aligned rows. <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> reach collection boundaries; <kbd>Space</kbd> toggles selection and <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the active card.
